P.Melodies — VST3 audio plugin for FL Studio (64-bit)

This installer copies the plugin to:
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\P.Melodies.vst3

After installing, open FL Studio and rescan your plugins:
    Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins

If FL Studio is open with the plugin loaded, the installer will ask you to close it.

To uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > P.Melodies (VST3).
